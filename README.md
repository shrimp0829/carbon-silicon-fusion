# Carbon-Silicon Fusion

> *Exploring the third path of evolution: from biological neurons to programmable meta-life.*

## About

This repository is my open notebook on **carbon-silicon hybrid intelligence** — a vision that carbon-based life (siphonophores, neural networks) and silicon-based computation (AI, neuromorphic chips) can merge into a new form of meta-life.


## What you'll find here

- `/papers` – Literature reviews and critical notes on siphonophore neuroscience, BCI, and neuroengineering.
- `/ideas` – Raw thoughts, hypotheses, and thought experiments (like the "Demon Roulette" game design).
- `/projects` – Reproducible code or simulation attempts (e.g., neural logic gates, EEG data analysis).
- `/meta` – Reflections on my own learning journey, philosophy of science, and career planning.

## Why open source?

Deep thinking should be shared, not hoarded. By making my notes public, I hope to:
- Find resonance with others who think about similar questions.
- Receive feedback and corrections.
- Document a path for anyone who wants to move from medicine to neuroengineering.
